Every host in your plan is someone we know

Montenegro has no shortage of operators. It does have a shortage of ones worth sending people to. We don't list a host until we've done the thing ourselves — which is why the names in your itinerary are families and guides, not call centres.

Met face to face, not found online

Every rafting guide, konoba and guesthouse in our plans has been visited in person. No aggregator listings, no commission-only partners we've never met.

You pay them, at their own price

Money goes straight from you to the host, in cash or by card, at the rate a local would pay. We never touch it and never add to it.

They answer the phone — and so do we

You get each host's number and a few words of context. If something goes sideways, we're an hour or two away, in the same country, in the same time zone.

Every trip on this site was put together exactly like that. Tell me your dates and it takes about five minutes.

Common questions

Everything you need to know

Is Montenegro safe for travellers?+
Montenegro is generally considered a safe destination, with a low crime rate and welcoming locals. As anywhere, sensible precautions apply. For adventure activities, I work with operators who follow proper safety practices.
Do I need a visa to visit Montenegro?+
Many travellers — including citizens of the EU, UK, USA, Canada and Australia — can typically enter Montenegro visa-free for short stays, but rules change and depend on your nationality. Always confirm current requirements with an official source or your embassy before booking. I'm happy to point you in the right direction.
How much does a typical trip cost in total?+
Typical trips range from €200 to €1,500 per person, depending on duration, activities, and accommodation style. The planning fee (€9–19) is separate and secures your plan once we've talked through your trip. All activity costs are paid at cost — either directly to operators or coordinated through me — with full transparency and no markup.
How does payment work?+
After you fill in the form, I reach out within hours to discuss your trip. Once we've shaped the direction together, I send a simple payment request (PayPal or bank transfer) to secure your plan. Activity costs are paid directly to operators or coordinated through me, fully transparent, no markup.
What if the weather changes my plans?+
Wherever possible, I build backup options into your itinerary. If a canyon day gets rained out, we can often switch to a wine tour or coastal boat trip. Montenegro's weather is generally pleasant from late spring through early autumn, though mountain and coastal conditions can vary — so flexibility is part of the plan.
Can you arrange accommodation too?+
Yes, from boutique hotels in Kotor's Old Town to mountain lodges near Durmitor. No markup, just honest recommendations based on what I'd personally choose.
What's the best time of year to visit?+
Spring (April–May) is perfect for rafting and hiking, with rivers full and crowds minimal. Summer (June–September) is peak season for the coast. Autumn brings wine harvest and golden landscapes. Winter offers skiing in Kolašin. There's no bad time, just different experiences.
Can you help groups and families with children?+
Absolutely. Tell me in your request and I'll build an itinerary for everyone. Montenegro has experiences for every pace. Not everything needs to be adrenaline.
How far in advance should I book?+
At least 2–4 weeks before your trip is ideal. Summer dates (July–August) fill quickly, so earlier is better. That said, I'll always try to make last-minute trips work if I can.
Still unsure? Send your dates and I'll tell you honestly whether I can make the trip worth it. No obligation, and no payment until we've agreed the direction together.
